# Style and Voice

## Existing guidance

The project's current prose guidance remains in `prose-guidelines.md`. Treat that file as an active source until its approved guidance is consolidated here.

## Narrative voice

To be established from approved manuscript exemplars.

## Dialogue

- Preserve distinct character strategies rather than relying on catchphrases.
- Prefer subtext and purposeful conflict to explanatory dialogue.
- Do not use dialect spelling unless explicitly approved.

## House style

- Use one-sentence paragraphs selectively to introduce a new beat or mark a genuine change in scene state.
- Within a beat, default to developed paragraphs. Avoid a repeated long-paragraph / one-sentence-paragraph cadence that gives every rhetorical conclusion equal typographic emphasis.
