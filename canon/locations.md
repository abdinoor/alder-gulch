# Locations

Record canonical geography, spatial relationships, physical descriptions, and scene-specific changes.

## Alder Gulch

- A roughly fourteen-mile gold-bearing gulch running toward the Ruby Valley in the Tobacco Root country.
- The placer discovery in May 1863 produced a rapid chain of camps and settlements sometimes called Fourteen-mile City.
- In December 1863 the gulch remained part of Idaho Territory; Montana Territory had not yet been created.
- The opening establishes its governing physical contrast: immense winter beauty and distance surrounding concentrated wealth with little effective institutional protection.

## Virginia City and Nevada City

- Virginia City is the largest settlement in the gulch at the time of the opening.
- Nevada City lies down-gulch to the northwest of Virginia City.

## Dempsey's ranch and the Tiebolt route

- Early accounts say Tiebolt went to Dempsey's ranch to retrieve mules after receiving payment for them.
- The exact route and site of the approved pursuit remain to be verified; Chapter 1 treats the chase geography as reconstruction rather than surveyed fact.
