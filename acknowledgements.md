# Acknowledgements

This book draws on the collections, scholarship, and public-history work of the Montana Historical Society. I am particularly grateful for its publication and preservation of *Girl from the Gulches: The Story of Mary Ronan*, Mary “Mollie” Sheehan Ronan’s recollections as told to and organized by her daughter, Margaret Ronan, and edited by historian Ellen Baumler. The memoir provides an invaluable—if necessarily retrospective—account of the Sheehan family, childhood, domestic life, work, schooling, and community in the early mining camps of Alder Gulch.

I am also grateful for Ellen Baumler’s research and interpretation in *The Women of Virginia City*, produced through the Montana Women’s History project. Its attention to Anne, Mary, Kate, and Ellen Sheehan, and to the homes and work of other women in Virginia City, helps recover lives too often left at the margins of accounts centered on miners, road agents, and Vigilantes.

These resources can be consulted through the Montana Historical Society:

- Mary Sheehan Ronan, *Girl from the Gulches: The Story of Mary Ronan*, as told to Margaret Ronan, edited by Ellen Baumler: <https://mths.mt.gov/education/docs/GirlFromTheGulchesPt1.pdf>
- Ellen Baumler, *The Women of Virginia City*: <https://montanawomenshistory.org/wp-content/uploads/2015/04/Women-of-VA-City_TourMap.pdf>
