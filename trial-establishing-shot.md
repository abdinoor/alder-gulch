# Trial Establishing Shot

Nevada City had no courthouse, so they made one in the street. A wagon served as the judge’s bench. The jurors sat close to a fire against the December cold. Lawyers and witnesses stood on a wooden box so the crowd could see them. Beyond the rough circle of the court, men packed the road shoulder to shoulder, coats buttoned high, hats low, pistols and rifles everywhere. It looked improvised because it was, but nothing about it felt casual.
