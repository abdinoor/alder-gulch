# Trailer Concept

The country. Vast, cold, beautiful.

Then hoofbeats.

Tiebolt riding hard. Looking back. Another rider gaining. Gold clenched against him.

Cut to black.

A body in the snow.

Then Nevada City filling with men. Horses tied three deep. Rifles, revolvers, heavy coats. A wagon serving as a bench. Jurors around a fire. Sanders speaking. Ives listening.

No one is unarmed.

A defense attorney presses a witness. A legitimate doubt enters the case. The crowd grows quiet—not because the trial is failing, but because it may be working.

Voices rise beyond the court:

> “We cannot let that happen.”
>
> “If he walks free, it’ll start the biggest fight this town has ever seen.”

Cut to Ives. Do not reveal whether he is convicted, acquitted, or executed.

Then faster images: road agents on horseback, a robbed stage, shuttered buildings, Plummer pinning on a sheriff’s badge, men conferring after dark, names being written down, a rope pulled through gloved hands.

The public danger: can the court determine whether Ives murdered Tiebolt?

The private danger: can the town survive either answer—and can these men risk another trial like this?

Black.

**ALDER GULCH**

Final image: a lone rider disappearing into the enormous landscape.

**There was law. There was just no one behind it.**
