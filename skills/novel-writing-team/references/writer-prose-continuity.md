# Writer, Prose & Continuity Protocol

## Required inputs

- Author request or submitted text
- Scene contract or clear scene purpose
- Target scene and neighboring prose
- Applicable narrative style guidance
- Participating character profiles and approved exemplars
- Current relationships, emotional states, and knowledge states
- Relevant timeline, canon, and research constraints

## Scene-drafting mode

Draft against the scene contract. Give every speaking character an immediate objective. Use disagreement, evasion, implication, silence, and action beats purposefully. Avoid explanatory dialogue between characters who already share the information. Maintain point of view and finish with the required change in scene state.

Do not introduce unsupported canon. Mark a fact-sensitive gap with a concise query rather than fabricating it.

## Dialogue-editing mode

Separate meaning, intent, subtext, voice, and canon. Preserve meaning and scene outcome by default. Revise language according to the speaker's worldview, status, relationship, emotional pressure, vocabulary, syntax, and current arc stage.

Use peculiarities conditionally. Do not maximize dialect, catchphrases, gestures, profanity, fragments, or repeated constructions. Ask whether another character could say the same line in the same way; if so, sharpen strategy or voice without making it theatrical.

Flag any proposed change to motivation, revealed information, relationship dynamics, or scene outcome for Story review.

## Prose-and-continuity mode

Improve clarity, rhythm, precision, imagery, emotional effect, grammar, usage, punctuation, typography, and house-style consistency. Check names, dates, locations, injuries, possessions, clothing, spatial relationships, chronology, clues, promises, and character knowledge.

Perform creation and editing as separate passes for generated prose. Do not polish text whose structure remains unsettled.

## Output

Return revised prose, material changes, continuity queries, new canon introduced, facts requiring verification, and proposed affected files. Do not edit canonical files.
