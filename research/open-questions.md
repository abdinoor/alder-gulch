# Open Research Questions

Track questions that require further evidence without blocking unrelated drafting.

## Nicholas Tiebolt biography

- Identify the exact Colorado mining district meant by Dimsdale's “Washington Gulch” and seek mining-district, tax, court, newspaper, directory, census, or naturalization records placing Tiebolt, Tbalt, Tiebalt, Thiebalt, or defensible phonetic variants there around 1861.
- Seek independent corroboration for Tiebolt's presence and Tom Baume's knife loan. Contemporary newspapers independently place John X. Beidler on the Washington Gulch freight route, but do not mention Tiebolt, Baume, Clark, or the loan.
- Distinguish the English-born Thomas Baume enumerated at Boulder City in 1860 from the Indiana-born Thomas Baum enumerated at Gold Hill, and determine whether the 1861 voter, September 1863 Central City passenger, October 1863 Denver salesman, and Montana Tom Baume can be connected or separated.
- Identify D. A. Butterfield's salesman through Denver directories, payroll, business, court, probate, newspaper, or manuscript records.
- Identify Dimsdale's William Clark through a Montana age, birthplace, middle initial, occupation, family member, partner, or business record; test the Sacramento Flatts retail dealer and the Oro City/California Gulch voters without merging them prematurely.
- Seek business, livestock, freighting, or mule-purchase records connecting William Clark to Butschy/Burtchy, Tiebolt, Baume, Beidler, Washington Gulch, or Nevada City.
- Determine how long Tiebolt was in Colorado, what work he did there, and whether he traveled directly from Washington Gulch to Alder Gulch or through other camps.
- Establish Tiebolt's exact birthplace, family, immigration date, native language, and arrival route. Do not reuse the rejected 1846 passenger candidates without new evidence that links them specifically to Colorado or Alder Gulch.
- Seek Nevada City residence, employment, claim, business, tax, burial, probate, or community records for Tiebolt and for Butschy/Burtchy and Clark.
- Determine whether Butschy/Burtchy, Clark, Tom Baume, John X. Beidler, William Herren, Elk Morse, or another documented associate can support a historically grounded Chapter IV recollection without inventing intimacy.
- Locate Tiebolt's reported burial place above Nevada and assess whether any cemetery, relocation, marker, or archaeological record survives.

- Determine which Sheehan details rest solely on Mary Ronan's 1929–32 retrospective account, which Margaret Ronan or later editors supplied, and which have independent support.

- Identify Ives's defense lawyers, the principal witnesses, jurors, finder of Tiebolt, early investigators, and people demonstrably close to Tiebolt.
